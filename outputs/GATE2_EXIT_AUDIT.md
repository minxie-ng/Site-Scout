The [manual comparison](GATE2_THREE_CLUSTER_COMPARISON.md) shows all three named groups and an all-abstain outcome. This is a valid screening result under `AGENTS.md`; it does not rank the three by market attractiveness. Punggol is the bounded **demo evidence region** because its nine exact HDB blocks have checked address and stock records and the audited source families cover relevant nearby context. It is not established as Singapore's best expansion region. The six wider northeast competitor comparators cannot be counted as Punggol outlets.

The original Task 2 plan mixed feasibility tests with desired **positive-use evidence upgrades**. `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` now retains the unmet upgrades as unchecked items: authenticated live OneMap Search, proof of a Phase 2 point, official future completion timing, 8–12 locally relevant outlet coverage, current exact-unit rent and Pilates use, and verified independent walking catchments. Their negative or incomplete audit results are what the Gate 2 feasibility check establishes; none is marked achieved. Punggol is selected for a reproducible demo comparison only, not because a location is ready for an owner to investigate commercially.

After Gate 2, Gate 3 must keep missing decision-critical facts out of calculations and prove `insufficient_evidence`, `no_go`, and conditional synthetic-scenario behavior. Live authenticated OneMap Search, a current exact-unit rent quote and permitted-use confirmation, local competitor geocoding/coverage, route geometry, and a verified future completion month remain explicit evidence upgrades before a real positive location decision. They are not prerequisites for checking whether the source system can fail safely.
