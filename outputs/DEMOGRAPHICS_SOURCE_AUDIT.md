# Gate 2 official demographics source sample

Retrieved: 6 October 2026. Source: Singapore Department of Statistics, [Resident Population by Planning Area/Subzone of Residence, Age Group and Floor Area of Residence (Census of Population 2020)](https://data.gov.sg/datasets/d_9e035622439b5d25a63d7ea0699c9451/view), dataset ID `d_9e035622439b5d25a63d7ea0699c9451`. Geographic sample: Punggol planning area and its named subzones, Singapore. Status: **official historical context; not a block-cluster decision record**.

| Field | Checked source statement | Decision treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reference period | Census of Population 2020; dataset metadata says data last updated 18 June 2021, update frequency 10 years, and catalogue entry created 22 October 2024. The page separately displays a 6 September 2026 update label. | Use 2020 as the observation period. Neither catalogue/page updates nor the 2026 retrieval date establish a newer population observation. Refresh if a newer comparable table becomes available. |
| Smallest geography | Planning area/subzone of residence under URA Master Plan 2019 boundaries. The table has no HDB block identifiers or coordinates. | A subzone is broader than the proposed few-block cluster. Do not assign its population or age distribution to specific blocks. |
| Sample API rows | The official `datastore_search` response returned 388 rows. Published table values for `Punggol - Total` are 174,450 residents and 8,330 aged 25–29; for `Punggol Town Centre`, 22,260 and 1,010 respectively. | These are **2020 published values for the named geographies**, not exact person counts, current residents, Pilates customers, or a forecast. Do not combine planning-area and subzone counts as independent populations. Numeric Punggol subzone totals sum to 174,460 while the published planning-area total is 174,450; do not reconstruct a total by summing them. The cause of non-additivity was not verified. |
| Dash-valued cells | Some subzone cells contain `-`, including Punggol Canal's total and age values in the checked response. | Preserve `-` as unavailable; do not coerce it to zero or infer its meaning without source documentation. |
| Rights/access | Dataset page states free personal or commercial reuse under the [Singapore Open Data Licence](https://data.gov.sg/open-data-licence). The licence requires conspicuous source acknowledgement and a link to the current licence and prohibits implying government endorsement. Public catalogue, API, and licence were retrievable without authentication on 6 October 2026. | Attribution is required in any product use. No paid access was needed for this sample. Recheck terms and API availability before automated ingestion. |

The sample was checked through the public [data.gov.sg datastore API](https://data.gov.sg/api/action/datastore_search?resource_id=d_9e035622439b5d25a63d7ea0699c9451&limit=400). The response was retained only as a temporary local audit file, not a versioned original in the repository. This audit therefore does **not** satisfy the Gate 2 usable-snapshot contract. A future normalized record must preserve the original response, dataset identity, reference period, geography, missing-value semantics, retrieval time, licence note, and integrity checks. It should be labelled `context_only` unless a separate defensible method links a statistic to a selected block cluster.

The figures cannot establish current occupancy, future residents of new flats, local willingness to pay, or demand for reformer Pilates. They can provide dated, broad population context once the target subzone and its boundaries are verified. A location recommendation cannot rely on this table as a block-level demand estimate.
