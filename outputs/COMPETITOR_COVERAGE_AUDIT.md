**Decision boundary:** only two outlet leads are in Punggol, and neither has a versioned original, source-bound coordinate, verified route/catchment, regular price, or measured overlap with the synthetic studio. The six regional comparators cannot fill that local coverage gap by being counted as if they served the selected blocks. The advertised am Pilates S$19 trial is brand-level introductory marketing, not a normal tariff. A [mall promotion](https://www.punggoldigitaldistrict.sg/shop/promotions/am%20Pilates%20-%2060%25%20off%20Reformer%20Pilates%20Class%20Pass) explicitly ran 1 September–31 December **2025** and is expired; do not display it as a 2026 price. This set is useful for a human investigation list, but cannot yet drive a numeric competitor score or positive block-cluster recommendation.
