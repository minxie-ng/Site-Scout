# Jev claim pilot — 8 October 2026

**Scope:** one opt-in, synthetic 30-case claim/passage pilot using the locked fixture in `tests/fixtures/claim-check.json` (20 tuning cases, 10 content-pinned held-out cases). This tests the combined deterministic precheck and Jev pipeline, not arbitrary source truth or real business decisions. The exact saved report is `outputs/JEV_BENCHMARK_2026-10-08.json`.

The completed run evaluated 30/30 cases in 9.774 seconds end to end. Ten were resolved by deterministic precheck; 20 reached OpenRouter Jev and all 20 reported the pinned served model `typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917`. The model's **raw** choice suggested `supported` for 9/9 expected-supported cases (3/3 held out) and for 0/21 expected-unsupported cases (0/7 held out). Only 11 of the 21 expected-unsupported cases reached Jev; 3 of 7 held-out unsupported cases reached Jev. Every positive raw suggestion had final verdict `review`, so automatic accepted support was 0/9. The conservative precheck baseline also accepted 0/9 and recorded no latency; therefore this pilot does not establish a production speed advantage. The model or precheck sometimes chose `not_established` where the fixture expected `contradicted`, and one `not_established` case received `contradicted`; three-class accuracy is not perfect.

The complete-run per-response `usage.cost` values sum to **USD $0.00037023**. A separate OpenRouter key has a non-resetting **USD $0.01** limit. Read-only key usage was $0 before attempts, $0 immediately after the first attempt timed out under Vitest's default five-second test limit, $0.00016527 immediately after the completed run, and **$0.0005355** on a delayed read after both attempts. The $0.00016527 difference between delayed account usage and the saved response sum is **unattributed to the saved case results**. It may involve the timed-out attempt or accounting lag; these readings do not establish the exact cause. No credential is in the report. The live test now writes only after a complete successful run, to a unique exclusive file, so an incomplete retry cannot overwrite the retained record.

Independent specification review and a separate quality review approved retaining and describing this **bounded synthetic pilot** after the cost and report-overwrite caveats were fixed. Neither review approved automatic positive acceptance, real-source reliability, a live site recommendation, or Gate 4 closure. All real site outcomes remain `insufficient_evidence`.

## Offline fallback timing and held-out error review

The saved pilot was re-analysed against a SHA-256 of **all 30** fixture cases; the report's row identity, provider transitions, aggregate counts, provenance, and total latency were validated before computing the tables. The analysis artifact is `outputs/JEV_FALLBACK_ANALYSIS_2026-10-08.json`. A single local timing run made 1,000 sequential passes over the 30 cases (30,000 calls to `precheckClaim`) in **25.838 ms**, averaging **0.000861 ms per precheck call** on this machine. This is an in-process kernel measurement excluding source retrieval, orchestration, UI, network, and any positive semantic check. It cannot be compared as an end-to-end speed ratio with the 9.774-second live Jev run.

On the **10 held-out synthetic cases**, the raw combined precheck-plus-Jev suggestions matched 3/3 supported, 2/3 contradicted, and 4/4 not-established labels; one expected contradiction was called `not_established`. There were 0/7 unsupported cases suggested as supported. The **full 30-case** raw table matched 9/9 supported, 4/9 contradicted, and 11/12 not-established labels; six labels were confused, including five contradictions called `not_established` and one not-established claim called `contradicted`. The conservative precheck alone suggested support for **0/9** supported cases and sent those claims to review. Final guarded Jev verdicts also accepted **0/9** support automatically, by design. Treat `review` as a safe abstention, not as a positive classification; a count of nonmatching labels that includes abstentions is not a business-accuracy score.

**Decision for this pilot:** Jev supplied useful positive *suggestions* in the synthetic set, but did not show that a business user gets a faster or more reliable final decision. Keep it optional behind the provider interface for source-bound semantic review; no raw model suggestion may enter deterministic calculations or authorize a positive site result. The next Gate 4 work is to connect reviewed source-bound claims, conflicts, and unknowns through the investigation flow and compare that workflow with the simpler baseline under the same evidence and tool budget. No production reliability claim follows from this pilot.
