## Gate 2 evidence contract

Additional 5 October 2026 audit notes: the [HDB virtual-tour portal](https://assets.hdb.gov.sg/community/punggol_point_cove_2/index.html) and its linked `assets/js/data.js` were directly retrievable, but neither identifies Block 442A or states a phase-to-block mapping. The [HDB 2024/2025 annual report](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/-/media/hdb-pulse/reports/annual-reports-and-financial-statements/HDB_AR2025.pdf) confirms Phase 2 completion in January 2025 but does not supply that mapping. These sources do not close the coordinate gap. The [HDB completion-status catalog entry](https://data.gov.sg/datasets/d_582672d2f972194786d01efe151892b7/view) is an aggregated historical series through March 2025, not a project-level future completion schedule. These observations have not been promoted to `usable` records.

A source becomes `usable` only when the repository contains:

- one original response or document snapshot;
- one normalized typed record;
- source URL, retrieval time, publication/effective date when available, and licence/access note;
- a validation that rejects a missing coordinate, ambiguous status, or unsupported decision claim;
- a documented fallback for outage, quota, or account failure.

The manually assembled comparison may proceed when named block membership, coordinates, at least one dated development event, a curated competitor sample, and editable economics are available for three HDB block clusters. A source scoped to a town or planning area is contextual and cannot be silently assigned to individual blocks. Rent and demand may remain assumptions if they are prominent, adjustable, and capable of producing `insufficient_evidence` or `no_go`.
