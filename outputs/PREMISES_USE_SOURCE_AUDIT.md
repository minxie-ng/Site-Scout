# Gate 2 official premises-use source sample

Retrieved: 6 October 2026. Official source: Urban Redevelopment Authority, [Residential Property and Business Owners' Guidelines — Business Owners](https://www.ura.gov.sg/Corporate/Guidelines/Development-Control/Planning-Permission/Change-of-Use). Geographic scope: Singapore premises generally; **not** a determination for any particular northeast unit. Status: **general due-diligence rule; exact Pilates premises use unverified**.

URA advises a business owner to check a premises' intended and allowable uses before committing to buy or lease it. Its checked Business Owners section says a premises cannot be used for a different purpose or activity without URA planning permission to change its use. The checked page did not identify group reformer Pilates as a specific use category, state the permitted use of the example 106 Hougang Avenue 1 listing, or supply a publication/effective date. Retrieval on 6 October 2026 is an audit date, not the rule's effective date.

| Question | Evidence-supported treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Does a commercial listing prove Pilates is permitted? | No. The example [Hougang listing](https://www.propnex.com/listing-details/964412/106-hougang-avenue-1) gives asking-rent information but does not establish the unit's last approved or allowable use, whether Pilates fits that use, or whether a change-of-use approval has been granted. Its floor description is also conflicting. |
| Does the rule apply to a named HDB block cluster? | It is a Singapore-wide due-diligence rule. It cannot turn an area or block cluster into an approved premises; a specific unit address and actual proposed activities are needed. |
| What must a human confirm before a lease? | Obtain the exact unit identifier and written evidence of its current approved and allowable uses; describe the group reformer Pilates activity and fit-out; confirm with the property owner and the relevant authority whether a change-of-use application or other premises approvals are needed, and obtain any required approvals before treating the unit as suitable. Other fire, building, accessibility, and business requirements were not audited in this one-source sample. |
| Can this source drive a recommendation? | It can drive a **blocking investigation/checklist item** for an exact unit. It cannot score a block cluster's commercial viability or mark any advertised unit approved. Missing or conflicting use evidence remains `insufficient_evidence` for a premises decision. |
